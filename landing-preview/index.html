How it works

Three steps.
Zero effort.

From a Telegram message to a submitted application — fully automated, no manual steps.

📨

Step 01

Job drops in Telegram

The bot monitors your Telegram job groups 24/7. The moment a job is posted, it's picked up and parsed automatically.

🧠

Step 02

Checks eligibility & tailors resume

Compares the job against your profile — batch year, experience, skills. If you're a fit, your resume gets tailored on the spot.

✅

Step 03

Form filled & submitted

Whether it's an email, Google Form, Greenhouse, Lever, or Workday — the bot fills it and submits. You get notified when it's done.

Features

Built for the
modern job hunt.

Every feature exists because job hunting is broken. This fixes it.

🌐

Universal Form Filling

Playwright-powered browser automation handles Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, Microsoft Forms, and any unknown ATS — not just pre-defined templates.

🎯

Eligibility Checker

Automatically skips jobs you don't qualify for. Checks batch year, years of experience, and skill overlap before wasting a single application.

📄

Resume Tailoring

Every resume is freshly generated from your LaTeX template, tailored to the specific job's keywords, role, and company. Not one-size-fits-all.

🔁

Duplicate Prevention

SQLite tracker remembers every application. Applied to the same company + role before? The bot silently skips it, no duplicates ever.

🔔

Telegram Notifications

Get pinged the moment an application goes out — or when a CAPTCHA blocks the bot and you need to apply manually. Always in the loop.

👤

Profile-Driven

One profile.json file is the single source of truth. Name, skills, experience, expected CTC — everything lives there, works for any user.
